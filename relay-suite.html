The RELAY Suite

Three tools, one idea.

Technology should make the relationships inside a school stronger: between a student and their teacher, a teacher and a parent, a centre and a home. Not thinner. Each product below takes that idea to a different part of a school.

Flagship productIn pilot

RELAY

Students explain their thinking in a space with no grade and no judgment. Where confusion appears in their own words, the teacher sees it in time to do something about it.
