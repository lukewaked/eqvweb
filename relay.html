Our approach

5-Step Engagement
Framework

Every school that partners with RELAY moves through the same structured process. Each step builds on the last: collaborative, trust-centred, and grounded in evidence at every stage.

1

Understand Context

We interview stakeholders across the school, including leaders, teachers, and where appropriate, students, to map challenges, priorities, goals, culture, attitudes, and existing policy. No two schools are the same; this step ensures we design for yours.

Stakeholder interviews · Policy audit · Culture mapping

2

Collaboratively Design

We co-design a bespoke engagement journey with your team, for both teachers and students. The journey moves through three phases: Foundation, Fluency, and Future, each building capability before the next begins.

Co-design workshops · Foundation → Fluency → Future

3

Pilot

We test with safeguards in place. Drawing on both qualitative and quantitative data, we refine the approach and measure real impact before any broader rollout. This is the phase pilot schools enter.

Controlled pilot · Data collection · Impact measurement

4

Implement & Adapt

Leader-led rollout with accountability champions embedded across departments. We review and refine continuously, building an ongoing relationship with the school rather than a one-off implementation.

Leader-led rollout · Accountability champions · Continuous review

5

Achieve Impact

Delivery of meaningful learning outcomes across the school. We workshop on trust — across every stakeholder relationship — and audit trust levels to confirm the impact is real and lasting.

Learning outcomes · Trust audit · Stakeholder review

What makes RELAY different

Built differently,
by design.

01
No Grade · No Judgment

Purely formative. Students explain honestly, not to perform for a mark. Removes the facade of understanding.

02
Student-Facing Pedagogy

Built on co-regulation and self-regulated learning, not productivity shortcuts. Designed for how students actually learn.

03
Cross-Curricular, K–12

One consistent tool across every subject and year level. Not locked to maths or a single subject area.

04
School-Context Customisation

Shaped to your curriculum, ethos, and student cohort, not a one-size-fits-all subscription.

05
AI Implementation Strategy

The plan behind the tool. Guardrailed AI outperforms unrestricted AI by 127% (Bastani et al., 2025). The variable is always design.
